How an answer is made and checked

The assistant can only say what the policy wording or the buyer's quote says. Claude drafts the answer; the page checks the draft in code before the buyer sees it. If any check fails, the buyer gets the policy text itself, not a summary.

1
Route the question. Claude sorts it into policy, advice, medical or other, and picks up to 3 clauses from a fixed list of 20. Advice and medical questions never reach step 2: they get a fixed reply and an offer to talk to a person.
2
Draft from the clauses only. Claude gets the question, the buyer's quote facts and the full text of the chosen clauses, nothing else. It must quote the exact words it relied on, or say it can't answer.
3
Check the draft in code. Every quote must appear word for word in the clause it cites; every number in the answer must appear in the cited clauses, the quote facts or the question; advice phrases are blocked; the answer must stay short.
4
Show or fall back. A draft that passes is shown with its quotes highlighted and the checks it passed. A draft that fails is replaced by the clause text and a 'Talk to a person' button.
